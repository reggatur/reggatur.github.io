Firm Central

Firm Central enables professionals to accomplish more for clients with less effort and stress. We do this by integrating with products used every day to increase firms efficiency, security and mobility. When looking for a cloud-based, legal practice management tool, Firm Central should be the center of the workday.

One of the most powerful components of Firm Central is the ability to upload documents and seamlessly integrate them with WestlawNext. This integration automatically reviews the entire document for current citation by adding links to WestlawNext including up-to-date KeyCite status flags.

Firm Central was constructed with the most important piece of a small law firm at the forefront, matters. The important parts of the matter are the people, deadlines and the content. The contacts, calendar and notes are all integrated with Microsoft Outlook to enable professions to work more efficiently and never miss a deadline. The content for matters is where Firm Central shines brightest above the competition. We offer one-click integration with tools like WestlawNext, Doc and Form Builder, and Drafting Assistant to name a few. The matter in Firm Central is where all these things come together in an organized way that helps everyone in the firm stay on the same page and have access wherever they are.

With a vast amount of sensitive information being used via the internet, security becomes a concern. Firm Central has the most robust security measures in place to store all the firm’s information in our state of the art data centers, encrypted and backed up nightly to give peace of mind that files are safe.

Thomson Reuters has been in the business of providing web-based products to law firms for almost 30 years, and we have a proven track record of providing quality products that add value. Regardless of what the future holds, you can trust us to continue to innovate and adapt our products to increase that value to our customers.

Simplify practice with Firm Central, the online practice solution for small law firms that connects day-to-day business functions by providing efficiency, security and mobility to help accomplish more for clients.

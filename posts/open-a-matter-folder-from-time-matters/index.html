Open a Matter Folder from Time Matters

Date: Aug. 19, 2020    Categories: document management

If you are using the Time Matters Document Management System, you can open documents directly from the Documents tab of a Matter or Contact.

But sometimes you may want to go directly to the folder containing documents for a Matter. That can be a time-consuming process, scrolling down through a long list of clients and repeatedly double-clicking into folders.

Time Matters has a shortcut for opening a Matter document folder in a few clicks.

To open the document folder of a Matter:

  1. Go to the Documents tab of the Matter.

  2. Right-click on any Document.

  3. Click: Open Containing Folder

Done! The folder opens in File Explorer (aka Windows Explorer). From there you can open any document by double-clicking on it.

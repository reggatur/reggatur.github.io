How to Link Time Matters to Microsoft Exchange 365

Date: Mar. 15, 2023    Categories: links , MS Office

Robert Gray of Legality Software published a step-by-step guide for linking Time Matters with Microsoft Exchange 365.

As of October 1, 2022, the old method for linking the two ceased working. Time Matters 16.6 or higher is required for the link to work. The setup process is not simple.

The free, 19-page PDF guide illustrates the setup steps with screenshots.

Many thanks to Robert and his team for creating this valuable resource for firms using Time Matters and Microsoft Exchange 365!

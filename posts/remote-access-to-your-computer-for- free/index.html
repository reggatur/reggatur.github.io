Remote access to your computer for free

Date: Jul. 11, 2026    Categories: utilities

Some people pay $349 per year for a remote control service to access an office computer from home. There is a good, free option: DWService.

Once installed, DWAgent allows unattended access to a far computer any time that it is powered on. The price? Free, or whatever you choose to contribute.

Since I discovered DWService in 2022, I have been very pleased with it.

A company making a remote control program needs to earn your trust. DWAgent from DWService.net is open source. It has been subject to the scrutiny of security researchers. While not a guarantee of perfection, that scrutiny provides a level of confidence.

DWAgent can be installed on a far computer (Windows, Mac or Linux) in unattended mode. It can also be used on demand allowing you to connect to someone else's computer while they are present.

You can install DWAgent on as many computers as you want. You can access one or more of them at the same time from your web-based control panel. DWAgent may be used for commercial purposes without charge.

The instructions for creating an account, downloading and installing DWAgent are presented in three videos of about four minutes each on the homepage, https://dwservice.net.

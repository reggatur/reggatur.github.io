See Daily Details in Calendar Views

Date: Oct. 8, 2017    Categories: calendar

In the Monthly and even Weekly views of the Time Matters Calendar, you may not see all your appointments. On a busy day, the later appointments don't fit into the box for that day.

When there are too many events to display all at once, Time Matters puts a triangle icon in the lower right corner of the box for that day.

Click the icon to pop up a window showing all the events for that day. Increase the size of the window by clicking and dragging one of the corners. Time Matters will remember the window size as a user setting.

Double-click any event to open and optionally modify it.

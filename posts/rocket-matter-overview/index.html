Introduction

Rocket Matter participated in the creation of our Cloud Practice Management Comparison spreadsheet. Detailed information about Rocket Matter features is available in the spreadsheet. Each participating vendor in our review of cloud practice management applications submitted additional material about their service. Here is the material from Rocket Matter.

Rocket Matter

At Rocket Matter, we have a simple mission: to make the lives of attorneys a whole lot easier. We work hard to identify the challenges that attorneys face on a daily basis and engineer solutions that’s fast and intuitive.

Attorneys can quickly capture time with Rocket Matter's unique Bill As You Work technology and run monthly invoices with just three clicks. Another popular time saver is the ability to upload templates of frequently created documents for one-click creation with Rocket Matter contacts and matters database. As lawyers work, user and matter dashboards shows their most pressing priorities at a glance.

Rocket Matter lets lawyers work with many of popular application they already use, integrating and synching with Dropbox, Gmail, Google Calendar, Evernote, Skype, Copy2Contact and Quickbooks. And for busy lawyers on the go, Rocket Matter offers robust native iPhone and Android apps and mobile sites for other smartphones and tablets.

And we know that software for lawyers needs amazing security. All Rocket Matter data is stored in a SAS 70 certified data facility and all traffic is encrypted over military-grade security, using 128-bit encryption.

Contacts

Copy2Contact Integration enables drag and drop email signatures and website contact information. All matters have a related contact section linking to contact section. Contacts can update their own information via the portal.

Matters/Cases

You are able to set viewing permissions to restrict matters. Calendar events can be shared with any contact record via portal.
Documents

You can share documents securely through the portal with any contact record, not just the client.

email

You can capture billable time from emails.

Timekeeping

There are nine separate ways to capture time easily within the program.

Accounting

QuickBooks integration does not require a manual export. The synchronization happens through Intuit with one click.

Support and Training

Unlimited support and variety of free training classes are available daily.

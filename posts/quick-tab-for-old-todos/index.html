Quick Tab for Old ToDos

Date: Jun. 8, 2005

A Quick Tab for ToDo's older than two weeks can remind you about tasks you may have forgotten. You can use this tab on your Time Matters ToDo List to deal with the problem of "out of sight, out of mind."

Add a Quick Tab for Two-Week-Old ToDo's

You can add a Quick Tab to your ToDo List to help you catch those that you may have overlooked.

  1. On your ToDo List, right-click on any Quick Tab. Click: Edit Quick Tabs.

  2. Click the Add button and fill in the General tab:

Type in a Name for the Quick Tab.

  1. Click on the Fields tab:

Use the Remove button to remove unnecessary fields such as: Status, Contact, Location...

  1. Click on the Filter and Sort tab:

Choose: Use Custom Search and click on the Combined button.

  1. Customize the Search window as follows:

In the Date From field, enter 1/01/2000. In the To field, enter a date that is two weeks before today's date.

In the Staff field, choose your name.

Check the box for: Dates are Relative to Today

Click on Not Done

  1. Click OK OK OK.

Now you have a Quick Tab that will show you all your ToDo's that are 14 or more days old and have not been marked Done.

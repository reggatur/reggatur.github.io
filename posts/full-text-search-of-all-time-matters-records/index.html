Full Text Search of All Time Matters Records

Date: Aug. 17, 2023    Categories: searching

Time Matters automatically indexes all the words in all your records in the Time Matters database. You can easily perform a full-text search from the Time Matters Main Menu.

In the Main Menu, click Search and then Global Search.

You go through several windows with Next, Next. Just one windows needs your input. Enter your search word or words here:

The results are in a list of clickable records. When you click one, you see it in view-only mode.

The Time Matters Global Search feature is a powerful tool for finding and reusing work you have done in the past. If you have forgotten the name of the client or case, you just need to remember a key word or two related to your work. Time Matters will find it for you!

Automate Your Fee Agreement

When you decide to make your law practice more efficient by automating some of your forms, you may want to start with your fee agreement form.

We have prepared a model Fee Agreement.docx form that you may download, modify, and use for free.

Creating your legal fee agreement (engagement agreement) is non-billable work, so it makes sense to do it as rapidly and efficiently as possible. The document assembly (automatic document creation) features of your practice management application enables you to do just that.

Since you need to have your client's name, address and e-mail address in the contact list of your practice management system, you might as well save time when creating the fee agreement by merging that information into your form.

Biggest Obstacle to Automating Forms

The biggest obstacle to automating your forms is not mastering the software. It is not finding the time to do it. The biggest obstacle is selecting each form and updating its terms. I can't tell you how many times document automation projects have stalled because lawyers hesitate to settle on a "final version" to automate.

Here is how you can hurdle that obstacle. Tell yourself two things:

  1. You can also tweak the terms of an automated document later. It is not cast in stone. Making some changes later is easy!

  2. You can modify the terms of a document to fit the needs a client. It is easy to change the terms after automatically creating a first version.

Fee Agreement Form

Maybe you don't like your current Fee Agreement form or Engagement Agreement form. If so, the accompanying Fee Agreement form may be useful.

Fee Agreement.docx

You may use this form freely and modify in any way you wish. Or you may copy terms from the form for use in your agreements.

The agreement is in Microsoft Word format with automatically numbered paragraphs. It contains variables, such as [CLIENT-NAME]and [PARALEGAL-RATE] that you should replace with merge variables supported by your practice management or document assembly software or with your own boilerplate text.

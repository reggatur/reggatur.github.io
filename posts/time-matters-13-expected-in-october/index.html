Time Matters 13 Expected in October

Date: Aug. 2, 2013    Categories: document management , products , updates

Time Matters 13 Adds Secure File Sharing

Here is how LexisNexis describes the upcoming release of Time Matters 13:

New in Time Matters 13! The ability to securely share or store documents in the cloud and access them on any device with full audit and tracking controls as well as the ability to revoke access. Our new Client Portal, available at no additional cost to Time Matters Annual Maintenance Plan subscribers, will enable firms to share and control documents with clients and third-parties across desktop, laptop and mobile devices - raising client service levels to new heights!

Secure, online file-sharing with clients and third-parties is just one of the new features included in Time Matters 13. Other productivity enhancing features include the ability to:

  • Preview bill layouts from Bill Layout designer and Bill Profile editor (Billing Matters Customers)
  • Color-code matters in Calendar, and attach to items you haven't billed

Additionally, Microsoft Windows Server 2012 support will be available. Keep on the lookout for your Time Matters 13 entitlement email targeted for October release.

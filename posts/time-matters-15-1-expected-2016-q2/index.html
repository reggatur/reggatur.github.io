Time Matters 15.1 Expected 2016 Q2

Date: Mar. 31, 2016    Categories: updates , upgrading

LexisNexis is expected to release Time Matters 15.1 in second quarter, 2016. Here are some of the improvements and updates we hope to see:

  • Updated integration with Microsoft Office 365 OneDrive
  • Adobe Acrobat DC add-in
  • QuickBooks 2016 link
  • WorldDox GX4 link
  • Fixes to our favorite glitches

I expect the upgrade process to be very smooth. I will test the new release and report here on my results.

For those of you who prefer to wait for point-one (.1) software releases before making the jump, this is good news!

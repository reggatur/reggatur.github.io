Save Time Entering Time

Date: Mar. 31, 2005    Categories: billing , calendar

If you use Time Matters with a billing program - such as PCLaw, Billing Matters, Timeslips or basic billing in Time Matters - you can enter time records directly from the Calendar.

The Time Matters calendar keeps you on top of your schedule. It can also save you work and time if you record billable hours. More importantly, using your Calendar to enter time can show you gaps in your schedule and remind you of work you might not have billed. That can put more money into your pocket.

Billing Matters Reports

Date: Nov. 14, 2013    Categories: billing , reports

This accompanying whitepaper describes some of the reporting capabilities of Billing Matters 9.0.

Billing-Matters-Reports.pdf

Though dated, the descriptions of the Billing Matters reports may be useful in understanding the options and content of many of the current reports in Billing Matters.

Contents of the whitepaper include:

  • Hours billed by timekeeper for a given period of time.
  • Hours billed by timekeeper for a given period of time by matter.
  • Billings by timekeeper for a given period of time.
  • Billings by timekeeper for a given period of time by matter.
  • Collected Fees by timekeeper for a given period of time.
  • Collected Fees by timekeeper for a given period of time by matter.
  • Accounts Receivable by timekeeper for a given period of time.
  • Accounts Receivable by timekeeper for a given period of time by matter.
  • Flat Fee Case Reporting
  • Profitability Reports

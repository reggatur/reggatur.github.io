Heads Up: Acrobat 10 and WordPerfect X5

Date: Dec. 3, 2010    Categories: document management , links

Acrobat 10 and WordPerfect X5 Not Yet Supported

As of 12/2/2010, LexisNexis does not yet support links in Acrobat 10 and WordPerfect X5 to Time Matters 10.

We expect that support for these third-party products will be included in Time Matters 11, expected in second quarter of 2011.

For information on other software, links and hardware requirements, see:

Time Matters Requirements Site

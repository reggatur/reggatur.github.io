Adobe Acrobat DC Fix for Time Matters 15

Date: Aug. 14, 2017    Categories: links

A recent automatic update of Adobe Acrobat DC broke the link to Time Matters 15. You can roll back to an earlier release of Acrobat DC by downloading an earlier release of Acrobat DC from the Adobe website.

Wells Anderson Receives CIC Choice Award

Date: Sep. 24, 2011    Categories: misc

For the second year in a row, LexisNexis Certified Independent Consultants voted for Wells Anderson to receive the CIC Choice Award. The award recognizes the consultant who is best at assistance, forum input, white papers, and technical capability within the LexisNexis Practice Management community.

The 2011 CIC Choice Award reflects volunteer work and the contribution of free materials and CIC Solutions Forum answers that benefit Time Matters, Billing Matters, and PCLaw customers and consultants.
